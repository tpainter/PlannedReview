Review the coordination of mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and fire-protection information with the construction plan drawings.

Check that:
- Equipment, panels, fixtures, risers, shafts, and service rooms are shown consistently between plans, schedules, diagrams, and details.
- Mechanical ducts, piping, cable trays, and electrical equipment do not visibly conflict with structure, doors, ceilings, stairs, required clearances, or one another.
- Plumbing fixtures connect to indicated supply, waste, vent, and riser systems where those systems are shown.
- Mechanical and electrical equipment has documented access, maintenance, and working clearances where applicable.
- Fire-protection devices, sprinkler zones, smoke-control components, and fire-rated penetrations are coordinated with architectural and life-safety information.
- MEP tags and referenced details resolve to the correct sheet and do not identify contradictory equipment or locations.

Do not infer concealed conditions that are not shown. If no MEP coordination errors are found, respond with "No MEP coordination errors found."
If errors are found, list each issue with the sheet/page number, location, affected systems, and the conflict or missing coordination information.
