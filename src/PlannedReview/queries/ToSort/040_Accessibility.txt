Review the drawings for accessibility and accessible-route coordination.

Check that:
- An accessible route is shown from accessible site arrival points and entrances to the occupied spaces and required amenities.
- Door clearances, maneuvering clearances, corridor widths, and turning spaces are dimensioned or otherwise adequately documented.
- Ramps, stairs, elevators, and changes in level are shown with consistent slopes, landings, handrails, guards, and detectable warnings where applicable.
- Accessible toilet rooms and fixtures have consistent layouts, clearances, grab bars, and approach spaces.
- Accessible parking, curb ramps, entrances, and path-of-travel information are coordinated between site and building sheets.
- Accessibility notes, symbols, elevations, and details agree across the plan set.

Do not assume compliance solely because an accessibility symbol is present. If no accessibility errors are found, respond with "No accessibility errors found."
If errors are found, list each issue with the sheet/page number, location, observed condition, and the missing, conflicting, or unclear information.
