Review the life-safety information shown throughout the construction plan document.

Check that:
- Occupant loads, occupancy classifications, and required exits are identified where applicable.
- Exit paths are continuous, clearly marked, and lead to an exterior exit or other indicated safe discharge.
- Exit doors swing and are located consistently with the egress path, and required exit signage or emergency lighting is shown when expected.
- Corridors, aisles, stairs, ramps, and exit doors have the required widths or clearly stated dimensions.
- Fire-rated walls, doors, shafts, smoke barriers, and penetrations are identified consistently between plans, sections, schedules, and details.
- Life-safety symbols and notes are not missing, ambiguous, or contradictory.

Use only information present in the document; flag items that require confirmation rather than inventing code values. If no life-safety errors are found, respond with "No life-safety errors found."
If errors are found, list each issue with the sheet/page number, location, observed condition, and the information or coordination needed to resolve it.
