Review the dimensioning on each plan sheet in the document.

Check that:
- Critical overall dimensions, room dimensions, and grid-to-grid dimensions are present where needed.
- Dimension strings are readable, use consistent units, and do not conflict with one another.
- Dimensions agree with the geometry, wall locations, openings, and stated areas shown on the drawings.
- Important offsets, clearances, and elevations are dimensioned or clearly controlled by a referenced detail.
- Duplicate dimensions do not create contradictory requirements.

Do not infer exact measurements when the drawing does not provide enough information. Report only issues supported by the document.

If no dimension errors are found, respond with "No dimension errors found."
If dimension errors are found, list each issue with the sheet/page number, the affected location or element, the conflicting or missing information, and a concise recommended correction.
