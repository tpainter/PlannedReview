Review the coordination of architectural and structural information in the construction plan drawings.

Check that:
- Structural grids, column lines, bearing walls, footings, beams, joists, and slabs align between plans, sections, elevations, and structural references.
- Column and wall locations do not conflict with doors, windows, stairs, shafts, rooms, or required clearances.
- Openings, penetrations, depressions, slab edges, roof edges, and framed features are identified consistently.
- Changes in level, foundation steps, retaining conditions, and support conditions are shown or clearly referenced.
- Structural notes, member tags, and details referenced on architectural sheets exist and match the depicted condition.
- Any apparent unsupported element, uncoordinated load path, or conflicting structural dimension is identified for professional review.

Report coordination concerns without making engineering calculations or asserting structural adequacy. If no structural coordination errors are found, respond with "No structural coordination errors found."
If errors are found, list each issue with the sheet/page number, location, affected elements, and the conflicting or missing information.
